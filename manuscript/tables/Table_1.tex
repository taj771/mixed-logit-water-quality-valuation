\small
Both models use covariates that interact WWF’s freshwater health scores with indicators for the spatial 
scale of the policy area (basin or sub-basin), further distinguished as local or non-local based on 
respondents’ home locations, to capture variation in marginal utility from water quality improvements 
depending on locality and spatial scale. Model 1, estimated as a Binary Logit (BL) model, assumes 
homogeneous preferences across individuals. Model 2, a Random Parameter Logit (RPL) model, accommodates panel data from repeated 
choice tasks, and substantially improves model fit. Robust standard errors are reported in the parenthesis.
